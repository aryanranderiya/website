# Privacy

This is the personal website of Aryan Randeriya. It collects no personal data and sets no tracking cookies of its own.

## What is collected

Two third-party analytics services are used to understand aggregate, anonymous traffic:

- **Google Analytics** (googletagmanager.com) — standard page-view and event metrics. Google may set its own cookies per their policies.
- **Rybbit** (self-hosted at as.heygaia.io) — a privacy-focused, cookieless analytics script that records anonymized page views without identifying visitors.

## What is not done here

- No accounts, so no user data is stored.
- No comment forms or newsletter sign-ups.
- No advertising or cross-site tracking.
- No selling or sharing of visitor data — there is no visitor data to sell.

If you email Aryan, the email and its contents stay between you and him; it is not added to any mailing list.

## Third-party content

Embedded widgets (Spotify, GitHub cards, maps) load from their own domains under their own privacy policies.

Questions about this page can go to [hey@aryanranderiya.com](mailto:hey@aryanranderiya.com).
