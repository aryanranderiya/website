# Aryan Randeriya

Designer & Developer. Founder & CEO of The Experience Company.

Currently building [GAIA](https://heygaia.io), a proactive personal AI assistant that acts before you even need to ask. The goal: every person in the world should have their own truly intelligent assistant.

The Experience Company's name comes from two things: the belief that its mission is to improve the human experience, and a deep care about the experience of every product it builds.

The fastest way to reach Aryan is email: [aryan@heygaia.io](mailto:aryan@heygaia.io).

Aryan is a tech nerd who has been tinkering with computers since a very young age. Born in the United Kingdom, based in India. He loves building products; a product guy but a developer and designer by heart. He loves music (he has a [Spotify playlist](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/1kDa0wKgm0baT3550xsURH) with 2000+ songs), movies, and food. He loves tinkering around with code, shipping things, and exploring new ideas.

Some things he would like to explore in the future by himself and with his company: Hardware programming, household robotics, personal companions, smart wearables & ar/vr.
