# About Aryan Randeriya

Aryan Randeriya is a developer, designer, and builder interested in hardware, robotics, and human-computer interaction. He is the Founder & CEO of The Experience Company, where he is currently building GAIA, a proactive personal AI assistant that acts before you even need to ask.

Born in the United Kingdom and based in Ahmedabad, India, Aryan has been tinkering with computers since a very young age. He describes himself as a product guy who is a developer and designer by heart: he loves building products, shipping things, tinkering with code, and exploring new ideas.

## What he is building

GAIA (heygaia.io) is an open-source personal AI assistant that proactively manages email, calendar, todos, workflows, and digital tools. The goal behind GAIA — and The Experience Company — is that every person in the world should have their own truly intelligent assistant, and that technology should improve the human experience of everyday life.

## Interests

Areas Aryan actively works in and wants to explore further: product engineering, AI agents, hardware programming, household robotics, personal companions, smart wearables, and AR/VR.
